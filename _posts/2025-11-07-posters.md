The **AI Poster Session 2025** showcased the most recent projects developed by members of [@hdspgroup](https://www.instagram.com/hdspgroup/), highlighting how Artificial Intelligence is being applied across multiple research areas, including **computational optics**, **seismics**, **medical imaging**, and more. 🤖💡

The session provided an academic space for **knowledge exchange and collaboration**, fostering dialogue between participants and strengthening our motivation to continue producing research and generating impact from **@uis**. 💚📊

✨ This event took place within the framework of the **5th Geophysics Week** ([@semana_geofisica](https://www.instagram.com/semana_geofisica/)) and was **funded through the Sustainability Fund 4439**.
